Future plan

Lab

A maker lab for 3D printing, rapid prototyping, CAD, and education is on the board for later. It is not open for booking or hire today.

We will only build this if Phyllux Tech turns into something that can honestly support a lab. Until then, treat this page as a direction, not a service menu.

If the lab opens later, the shape would likely include:

  • 3D print capacity (planned, not bookable now)
  • CAD and product design help
  • Rapid prototyping and light assembly
  • 3D scan and reverse engineering
  • Maker education workshops

Curious for the long game? Use contact with subject Lab interest. That is interest only, not a booking.
